Delicious Chicken Biryani

A flavorful and aromatic Pakistani/Indian dish that combines tender chicken, fragrant rice, and a blend of traditional spices.

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice - 2 cups
  • Chicken - 1 kg
  • Onions - 2 large
  • Tomatoes - 2 medium
  • Yogurt - 1 cup
  • Spices:
    • Garam masala - 2 tsp
    • Turmeric powder - 1 tsp
    • Red chili powder - 1 tsp
    • Cumin seeds - 1 tsp
  • Cooking oil - 1/2 cup
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves

Cooking Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices for 1 hour
  2. Cook rice with whole spices until 70% done
  3. In a separate pan, fry onions until golden brown
  4. Add marinated chicken and cook until tender
  5. Layer partially cooked rice and chicken mixture
  6. Steam on low heat for 20 minutes
  7. Garnish with fried onions and fresh herbs
